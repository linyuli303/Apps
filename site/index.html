    ▼ Connection and privacy

    Authorization takes place on Spotify. MyApps never receives your Spotify password and does not use a client secret.

    Connecting will temporarily store a PKCE login transaction in tab-scoped browser storage. Its validity is limited to ten minutes; it is removed when consumed or explicitly cancelled. Access and refresh tokens remain in page memory.

    Permissions cover followed artists, playlists, saved-song status, Like/Unlike, playback, and the account permissions required by Spotify's player integration. Like/Unlike changes your actual Spotify library only after an explicit action.

    Reloading loses the in-memory connection. Disconnect clears MyApps' connection but does not sign you out of Spotify globally or revoke the app grant in your Spotify account.

    Spotify services may use their own cookies and browser data. This is not a cookie-free or cache-free playback mode.
